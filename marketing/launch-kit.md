# Launch kit — LaunchCrew

This is a working launch kit, not a guarantee of stars, press, inbound emails, or adoption. Customize every draft to match the repository's actual state. Do not claim tests, users, metrics, or features that have not been verified.

## Positioning

**One-liner:** A practical startup-team workflow kit for Codex CLI and ChatGPT: spec-first development, focused specialist agents, and quality gates packaged as portable skills.

**Audience:** developers using Codex CLI, small startup teams, maintainers who want repeatable AI-assisted engineering, and ChatGPT users who want structured workflows rather than one giant prompt.

**Problem:** AI sessions are powerful but drift when requirements, tests, decisions, and progress exist only in chat history.

**Promise:** Leave durable artifacts, work in verifiable slices, and use specialists only when they help.

**Proof to show before launch:** a clean clone and quick start, skill metadata validation, ChatGPT bundle generation, a small demo spec, and a real example of one workflow improving a task. Add screenshots only if they are authentic and current.
