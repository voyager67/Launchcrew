# Startup operations workflow

1. Establish stage, current product, audience, team capacity, budget/runway assumptions, constraints, and the decision to make.
2. Separate observed metrics from estimates. For every key number capture source, time window, definition, and confidence.
3. Connect the current bottleneck to a small set of leading and lagging indicators. Avoid metrics that can be improved while user value worsens.
4. Prioritize opportunities using impact, evidence strength, effort, risk, reversibility, and time-to-learning. State what is deliberately not being done.
5. Define a short operating rhythm: weekly outcome, owner, leading indicator, risks/blockers, and decisions needed. Keep it proportional to team size.
6. Review unit economics, cost drivers, pricing, customer acquisition, retention, and cash needs only when relevant and with explicit assumptions.
7. Create scenarios for uncertain inputs rather than a single fake-precise forecast. Identify trigger points for changing course.
8. End with decisions, owners, deadlines, review metrics, and unresolved questions.

## Output

- Current state and evidence quality
- North-star outcome plus guardrail metrics
- Prioritized bets and rationale
- Capacity/budget assumptions and scenarios
- Operating cadence and decision log
- Risk triggers / next review

## Guardrails

Do not invent financial facts or present projections as certainty. For accounting, legal, tax, fundraising terms, or regulated matters, flag where professional advice is needed.
